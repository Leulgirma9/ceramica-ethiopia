Our Story

Ceramica Ethiopia is the nation's leading supplier of premium ceramic tiles, porcelain, sanitary ware, faucets, and complete bathroom solutions. We serve architects, interior designers, contractors, government agencies, and procurement officers with the finest materials from world-class manufacturers.

Our showroom in Addis Ababa showcases thousands of products, and our team of experts is always ready to assist with product selection, specification, and procurement support.
